How to order

A full build, a half-baked knife, or something already finished. The old site separated them on purpose.

Frame locks. The 247, Busker, Soloist, Rainmaker, Swish, and Whippersnapper each had a build form. You looked at the build assistant first. The shop wrote back within two business days. The Whippersnapper frame-lock books were closed when that page was current. The bolster lock replaced them.

Liner locks. Wayfarer Classic, Wayfarer Compact, and Rainmaker Classic are full custom, and the option list was too long for a form. You described the knife. The old page had the books opening at the end of October 2021. Treat that date as history.

Half baked. A narrower set of choices on a knife that already had a direction. A couple of days to finish once you chose. The half baked page explains it.

Ready to ship. Experimental builds, the first of a new idea, or a knife that simply came out well. The old shop page mixed those with a sold archive. When the main site has been in maintenance, finished knives have been posted on The Olamic Table.
