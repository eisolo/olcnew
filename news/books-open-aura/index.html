Books open on the all-titanium automatic

April 1, 2025: Olamic opened orders on its first automatic, an all-titanium button lock. The announcement pointed people at the model page.

Specs from that page: 3.5 inches of CPM 20CV, 8.5 inches overall, 5 inches closed, 4.4 ounces. The Aura page has the rest. This preview does not know whether the book is still open. Ask the shop.
