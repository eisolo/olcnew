After it ships

Support

Lifetime warranty on Olamic knives. If something is wrong, write before you ship.

Every Olamic knife is covered by the lifetime warranty against defects in materials or construction. The warranty follows the knife. It is not limited to the first owner. It does not cover abuse, accidents, loss, theft, or work someone else did to the knife.

Spa service is free. At the shop's discretion it can include a new lockbar insert, detent ball, pivot, screws, clip ball, or bearings. They will also reset detent strength, lock engagement, and centering, then clean the knife, oil it, and look it over. Sharpening is part of the spa unless you ask them not to. Upgrades get a spa pass by default.

Email support@olamiccutlery.com. The ship-to address is Olamic Support, 650 S Bridge St, Suite A, Visalia, CA 93277. Use a service with tracking. Do not ship a knife until a service request is in. International shippers should ask about the shop's discounted rates before they buy a label.

The old support page also linked assembly notes for the Swish and the Busker. Those steps are not in the export used here, so this preview does not invent them. Ask support for the current sheet.

For a customization, a trade-in, or a plain question, use the note below or the service form.
